— Our Story

A small roastery in the hills.

Cup'O'Chino was born in a small roastery in the hills of Coorg, where the air smells of rain and fresh-ground coffee. We started with one roaster, two hands, and an obsession with the perfect extraction.

Every batch we roast is traceable to a single farm. We believe the story of a coffee matters as much as its taste. Our farmers are partners, not suppliers.

Whether you're a first-time espresso drinker or a seasoned barista, Cup'O'Chino is your daily ritual. Pull up a chair. The coffee is almost ready.

— The Process

The Craft Behind Every Cup.

01

Sourcing

We source exclusively from single-origin farms across Coorg, Chikmagalur, and Araku Valley. Every lot is cupped and approved by our head roaster before it enters our facility.

02

Roasting

Our drum roaster runs at precise temperature curves developed over 3 years of testing. Each origin gets its own roast profile — never a one-size-fits-all approach.

03

Grinding

Fresh grinding happens within 30 minutes of brewing. We use calibrated burr grinders set to the exact micron for each brew method.

04

Brewing

Every extraction is measured. Dose, yield, temperature, and time — all dialled in to within 0.1 grams. This is not guesswork. It is craft.
